## Brand position

Sande Tech is a software development company (Lilongwe, Malawi) that works three ways, and the site must make all three obvious within the first screen:

1. **Our products** — software we build and run ourselves (VepiFind, Belo, Hospr).
2. **Remote tech team** — our engineers working inside a client's company, tools and sprint.
3. **Tech agency** — client projects delivered end to end.

Headline: "We build our own products. And we'll build yours."

## Signature

The hero "hub": the Sande Tech mark sits in a glowing core with orbit rings, wired (with plus-nodes) to three floating cards — one per way of working. Wires draw once on load; this is the only orchestrated motion. A faded "SANDE" word and a perspective grid floor sit behind it.

## Layout

Every section is a rounded framed panel on a cool grey page (#DFE6F2), alternating dark (hero, products, process, footer) and light (modes, team, agency, contact). Content max-width 1360px.

## Components

- Buttons are pills with a circular arrow dot on the right. Light pill on dark; night pill with ice dot on light.
- Nav is a translucent pill group; "Start a project" sits beside it.
- The remote-team "Build your team" picker pre-fills the contact form.
- The contact form opens the visitor's email app with the enquiry composed (no backend yet).

## Don'ts

No fake testimonials, client logos or stats. No numbered markers except the real sequence (process steps). Respect reduced motion.
